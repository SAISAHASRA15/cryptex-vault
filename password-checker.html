Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about password security and best practices.

What makes a password strong?

A strong password contains a combination of uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, special characters, and is at least 12 characters long.

How often should I change my passwords?

It's recommended to update important passwords periodically and immediately if you suspect an account has been compromised.

Should I use the same password for multiple accounts?

No. Using unique passwords for each account reduces the risk of multiple accounts being compromised if one password is exposed.

What is two-factor authentication (2FA)?

Two-factor authentication adds an extra layer of security by requiring a second verification method in addition to your password.

Are password managers safe to use?

Yes. Reputable password managers securely store and generate strong passwords, helping you avoid weak or reused credentials.

How can I protect my accounts from hackers?

Use strong passwords, enable 2FA, avoid phishing scams, keep software updated, and never share your login credentials.
